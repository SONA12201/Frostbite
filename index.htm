About Frostbite

Cold Chain Logistics Designed Around Cargo Integrity

Frostbite represents a logistics business concept that coordinates storage, packaging and temperature-controlled transportation for products that need special handling.

  • What we do: Cold storage and temperature-controlled logistics.
  • Target customers: Pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, laboratories, food businesses and distributors.
  • Proposed service area: India, subject to operational coverage.
  • Value proposition: Product-specific handling, shipment visibility and planned exception management.
