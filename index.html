About Frostbite

Protecting Products Through a Smarter Cold Chain

Frostbite focuses on the logistics requirements of products that must remain within defined temperature limits throughout storage, handling, and transportation.

Our proposed model brings together cold storage, refrigerated transport, packaging, shipment visibility, and delivery coordination to support reliable supply chains.

  • Target customers: pharmaceutical companies and distributors
  • Hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, and life-sciences businesses
  • Food businesses and other temperature-sensitive cargo owners
  • Proposed service area: Vadodara, Gujarat, with expansion across India
  • Value proposition: product-focused handling and shipment visibility
