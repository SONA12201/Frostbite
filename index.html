About Frostbite

Keeping Temperature-Sensitive Supply Chains in Control

Frostbite focuses on the planning and coordination of logistics for goods that need controlled temperatures throughout storage and transportation. Our proposed model combines appropriate handling, cold-chain planning and shipment visibility.

  • Pharmaceutical and healthcare supply chains
  • Biotechnology and laboratory products
  • Food and other temperature-sensitive goods
  • Storage, packaging and transport coordination
